About Us

At SUMO, we serve comfort food made fresh, from crispy chicken wings to sizzling Japanese hibachi, packed with flavor and ready when you’re hungry.

Sumo Hibachi and Wings brings bold, made-to-order flavor to Oakbrook Parkway in Norcross, GA. Wings hit the sauce the second they leave the fryer, hibachi is seared on a screaming-hot flat top, and Philly sandwiches are stacked and pressed while you wait. Our kitchen keeps it straightforward — fresh ingredients, generous portions, and prices that make coming back easy. Whether it's lunch for one or two hundred wings for the whole crew, we've got you covered.
